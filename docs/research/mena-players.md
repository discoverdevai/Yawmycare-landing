## 6. Compliance reference (for claims discipline)

| Law | What we confirmed | Source | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Egypt PDPL 151/2020 | Executive Regulations issued Nov 2025 (Decree 816/2025). One-year grace ends around **Nov 2026**. Regulator: Personal Data Protection Center. Licences/permits required, cross-border transfers restricted (adequacy, approval or consent) | Al Tamimi, CMS, Kennedys (via WebSearch, details disputed between sources) | Egypt: do not claim "compliant" or "licensed" until counsel confirms. Regime is going live around the launch window |
| Saudi PDPL | In force since 14 Sep 2023 (M/19), SDAIA supervises (per EduX page, vendor-stated). Children's data treated as sensitive | EduX page | A KSA claim of "data stays in the Kingdom" needs actual KSA hosting |
| ZATCA Phase 2 | Mandatory e-invoicing in KSA. KinderLink and EduX both reference it | Vendor pages | Only relevant if Yawmy sells to KSA nurseries. Not an Egypt concern |

## A. How MENA buyers evaluate and buy

| Observation | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp is the default contact channel | InstaKidz floating button with prefilled message, KinderLink wa.me, EduX hero CTA, Wakeb "talk to sales" | A floating WhatsApp is table stakes. Hadana, Skolera and Classera lack it prominently, which is a visible gap |
| Demo call comes before trial | Hadana, InstaKidz, KinderLink, EduX all route trials through a call. Self-serve (Wakeb, Nursery Master) is the minority | The buyer is an owner-operator who wants a human. A demo-first funnel is normal. Reduce form friction, offer WhatsApp as the alternative |
| Price asked first, rarely shown | Only Hadana and Wakeb publish. KinderLink, InstaKidz, EduX, Skolera, Classera hide | Public pricing is a differentiator. "One flat fee" with no number invites the same question every time |
| Arabic quality is tested, not assumed | LumiNest guide: check teacher and parent apps are RTL, not just translated menus | Show the real Arabic parent app UI in the hero. Do not show translated English mockups |
| Local references matter | InstaKidz names nurseries and governorates. KinderLink uses "+100" and one logo. Skolera uses school logos | Named Egyptian nurseries with city, even 3, outweigh global stats |
| Money handling | Buyers ask about InstaPay, cash, transfer, Fawry (LumiNest guide, Hadana) | Fee collection is the main owner pain. Recording off-app payments matters as much as card checkout |
| Switching cost fear | LumiNest guide: data import, training, export on exit, cancellation terms | Free migration and "export anytime" lines reduce risk |
| Parent app must be free to parents | InstaKidz badge, LumiNest checklist | State it explicitly |

## B. Arabic landing-page best practices (drawn from the above, plus standard practice)

| Area | Practice | Evidence / note |
|---|---|---|
| RTL polish | Logical properties, mirrored icons with direction (arrows, progress), but do not mirror phone-status or logos | Already in `CLAUDE.md`. Hadana showed no direction bugs, a useful bar |
| Numerals | Pick one system per locale and stay consistent. Egypt: Western digits are common in B2B web, so default to **Western digits in AR** (matches prices, phone and times) unless design wants Eastern | Hadana mixed both: a visible flaw |
| Mixed script | Wrap Latin brand names, prices and EGP in `dir="ltr"` spans or `<bdi>`. Add spaces between Arabic and digits (KinderLink had "20ساعة") | Observed defect |
| Typography | Use Expo Arabic (planned) with a system Arabic fallback. Arabic needs about 1.5 line-height (already in the tokens). No letter-spacing on Arabic. Avoid uppercase styling | Standard practice |
| Tone | **Mixed**: MSA for trust and legal text (security, FAQ, pricing terms), light Egyptian hooks in hero subline and CTAs ("اتكلم مع فريقنا" on Hadana). For KSA/GCC visitors, keep MSA or Khaleeji-neutral | Hadana, InstaKidz do Egyptian. KinderLink, Skolera use MSA |
| Hooks to consider | Parent-worry hooks ("إيه اللي عمله ابني النهارده؟") work for Egypt but tag as Egyptian dialect so it can be swapped for Gulf | Our inference, not tested |
| Bilingual toggle | Top-end of the header (start-to-end, trailing in both directions), labelled in the target language ("English" / "العربية"). Persist choice, and auto-detect with `Accept-Language` on first visit | Observed on Hadana (5 languages), Skolera, KinderLink |
| Language default | Arabic first for Egypt. Do not translate EN to AR 1:1; write AR natively | Nursery Master shows the risk of leftover English |
| Forms | Mobile-number first, WhatsApp-reachable, optional email. Governorate dropdown (InstaKidz) plus child-count range qualifies leads. Check duplicates in the list | Observed |
| Dates and times | 12h with "ص / م" in AR. Avoid "14:00PM" (already flagged in PLAN) | PLAN.md |
| Proof formats | Named nursery + city + role, photo, short quote | Observed |

## C. Gaps Yawmy Care can own

| Gap | Why it is open | How to claim it |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparent flat-fee pricing in EGP** | Almost every competitor hides price or charges per child. Hadana is per-child tiers, LumiNest from EGP 1,800 | Show the actual number or a calculator. "One flat fee, paid by the nursery, parents never pay" |
| **Verifiable "real" proof** | Hadana none, Wakeb has "0+" counters, InstaKidz ratings unsourced | Three named Egyptian nurseries and a "first-10 partner" scheme. Show sources for numbers |
| **Nursery + international school in one** | Nursery tools (Hadana, KinderLink) and school LMS (Skolera, Classera) are separate | The "international school: talk to us" lane. Bridge nursery to KG to school |
| **Teacher experience** | Competitors pitch owners and parents. Teacher app only lightly covered | "Takes a teacher 10 seconds" demos |
| **Honest trust page** | EduX is the only one with explicit residency and PDPL language, KSA only. Nobody does it for Egypt | An honest security and data page (what is stored, where, who sees it), not a badge |
| **WhatsApp plus the app, not instead of** | Nursery Master attacks WhatsApp groups. Wakeb sends WhatsApp bus alerts. Parents live on WhatsApp | Position as "the thing your WhatsApp group should have been" without claiming Meta-API integrations we may not have |
| **Day-in-the-life storytelling** | Only KinderLink does it (KSA) | Yawmy's timeline section is a differentiator in Egypt. Keep it and make it Arabic-real |
| **Commitments / no-lock-in** | Only LumiNest speaks to exit and export | "Five things we will not do to you" is already unique. Surface export and cancel in the hero area |

## D. Risks and claims to avoid

| Risk | Detail | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Data residency | Wakeb contradicts itself on server location. EduX does it properly for KSA | Claim a region only if hosting is actually there. Otherwise say "encrypted, access-controlled" and link to a data page |
| Egypt PDPL | Regs issued Nov 2025, grace ends about Nov 2026, licences and cross-border rules apply | No "compliant with Egyptian PDPL" until counsel signs off. "Built with the law in mind" is also a claim. Prefer facts |
| Saudi PDPL / ZATCA | Applies only if KSA sold | Do not mention KSA compliance unless that market is in scope |
| "Bank-level security", "256-bit SSL", "100% safe" | Overused, unverifiable | Avoid superlatives. State specific controls |
| Unsourced stats and ratings | InstaKidz, KinderLink, Wakeb all publish unsourced numbers | Use only counts we can back with a name or date |
| Children's photos | Both EduX and LumiNest treat these as sensitive | Say who can see photos, retention, and that parents consent |
| Payment gateway names | Fawry, Paymob, InstaPay | Name only gateways that are live. LumiNest's candid "coming soon" is a good model |
| Testimonials | PLAN.md already flags that they need real, approved names | Written approval on file before publishing |
| Placeholder SLA | PLAN.md "[X] working hours" | Replace with a real, honoured number before launch |
| Fake counters/animated "0+" | Wakeb | Render counts server-side or leave them out |
| Forms asking passwords for a demo | Classera | Never |

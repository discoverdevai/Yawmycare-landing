# Yawmy Care voice cheat sheet (one page; full guide in `docs/BRAND.md`)

Calm, experienced friend. Warm, plain, specific, honest. English leads; Arabic is supported, not the headline.

| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| Run your nursery from one app, not WhatsApp and Excel. | Revolutionise your childcare ecosystem. |
| Parents never pay. One flat monthly fee, paid by the nursery. | Trusted by thousands of happy nurseries. |
| We set it up with you, within 48 hours. | Most nurseries are set up in 20 minutes. |
| Book a free demo (30 minutes). | Submit / Get started / Watch Demo |
| We are new, and that is why your voice counts. | The number one nursery platform. |
| Noted as it happens, not from memory at half past four. | AI-driven insights. |

Hard rules: write "Yawmy Care" / «يومي كير»; sentence case; no emoji or exclamation marks; no security or data claims (decision 38); no invented numbers, customers, quotes, founders; Western digits; Arabic light-MSA, teachers feminine (المعلمات), brand masculine; AR strings are draft until a native reviewer signs off.

Tagline: Every child's day, in one place. / يوم كل طفل، في مكان واحد. [AR draft, pending native review]
